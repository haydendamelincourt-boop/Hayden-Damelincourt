How I got here

Four Interests, One Direction

My passion for investing does not come only from a love for numbers and the stock market. Instead, I find finance, specifically private equity, to be the perfect intersection between my interests, my constant search for knowledge, and passion for business.

I'm a Finance and History double major at Florida State University, an honors student, a researcher, and a member of Noles on Wall Street Program. All this involvement leads to one goal, to start my own private equity firm.

What shaped me

The Interests Behind The Résumé

01

The Ocean

I scuba dive, free dive, and spent a summer helping save sea turtles on my local Florida beaches. Diving that often means watching climate change damage reefs and nesting grounds firsthand, with every year the damages becoming more evident.

That's what led me to Sailplan, where I worked with clients including the U.S. Navy, U.S. Army, and Disney Cruise Line to cut their emissions footprint by nearly 15%. I learned fundraising and business development, but more importantly, I saw the work I was contributing to making a difference in my local waters. This passion for protecting marine environments is why I'm serious about investing in renewable energy and climate tech.

02

Soccer

I've played soccer my whole life, up through the semi-professional level. Soccer is what brings my family together, and it is how I have met some of my best friends. It taught me how to lose, work hard, and how to lead teams. It is through soccer that I found myself to thrive in competitive team atmospheres.

This same atmosphere is matched in private equity. Deals fall through and models can be wrong, but in the end it is how you respond to these setbacks. Soccer trained me to take the loss, learn from it, and show up again even stronger.

03

Entrepreneurship

In high school, I co-founded myKickstrt with two other students. We raised over $10,000, drew interest from major Florida universities and companies, and became stewards for a wider program promoting high school entrepreneurship across North America. myKickstrt connected students from underprivileged areas with internships, jobs, and community service opportunities, giving these students real résumé lines that closed the disparity gap in college applications.

Freshman year at FSU, I worked with CollegeTown AI, centralizing student-facing social, educational, and local business promotions into one platform. These experiences in entrepreneurship, paired with my passions investing, traveling, and protecting nature are what make me want to found my own firm.

04

Travel

I've traveled since I was a kid, visiting Canada, the U.S., Costa Rica, France, and many more countries. I love adventure trips, such as hiking, skiing, the cliff worth jumping off. In addition, I've also worked summers in local hotels, seeing hospitality from the operating side, not just as a guest.

My passion for adventure hospitality and the outdoors is threatned by the impacts of pollution. I want to take my understanding of hospitality and interest in climate tech to invest in a more sustainable travel indsutry that preserves nature while still allowing for breathtaking adventures.

Now it is clear that what interests me directly correlates to me wanting to do the buy-side. I don't just want to chase returns. I want to succeed while working and investing in what I am passionate about.

Project Alpha

A buy-side thesis on a full-service Mechanical, Plumbing & Electrical (MP&E) contractor with a 50+ year operating history across the Southeast. We built the case around three pillars:

  • Strategic M&A into a highly fragmented market
  • Operational centralization to unlock $3–4M in cost synergies
  • Organic growth from a wave of infrastructure and industrial investment across the region

What I'd bring to Recalc

Community, Mentorship, and Where This Goes Next

Outside of school and work, I am a mentor with FSU's Investment Banking & Alternatives Organization and The FSU Consulting Group. Every community I am a part of, I try to give back. Back home, I saw that some students didn't have equal opportunities, so I founded myKickstrt. When I join communities, I stick around, and during the summer I help to coach at my local soccer club, serving as a role model for the younger kids.

I give back to communities that give to me. I know that Recalc and the mentors want to help me, and that is exactly what I want to reciprocate. With soccer, the coaches and older players taught me, and now I do the same for the younger kids. Through the club, the mentors last year helped answer all of my questions, and now I'm doing the same for the freshmen.

I have talked to members of the Recalc community, and here's what makes the program stand out to me:

01
Real modeling training

Katie and Suzanne train actual private equity teams on how to model, and that same hands-on training is what Recalc members get access to.

02
Summer meetups

Every summer, Recalc runs meetups for interns to actually meet people across the program in person, not just over a screen.

03
A Lively Slack

People are constantly organizing pop-up events in the Slack, just to get to know more people in the program.

04
Genuine advice, not small talk

Members of the community genuinely cares about each other, every conversation turns into real advice from people who have experienced different things in the industry and gone through different paths, which helps you better understand the problems and opportunities you run into.

That's what I'd bring to Recalc:

  • Curiosity to learn
  • A devoted ambition to succeeding not as an individual but as a community
  • A habit of building instead of waiting

I'm a sophomore with a dream of running my own private equity firm. I want to join Recalc to help make that dream a reality while helping the rest of the community also meet their goals!
